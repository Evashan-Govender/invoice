# Sambe Consulting Corporate Identity Guidelines

### 1. Brand Philosophy and Responsibility
*   **The Brand Promise:** The brand represents a "promise" made as one unified business. It focuses on providing the best engineered solutions to help customers improve productivity and profitability.
*   **Consistency:** Success requires absolute consistency in the look, feel, tone, and message across all official colors and logo usage to ensure a strong brand and a consistent face to the customer.
*   **Responsibility:** All employees, distributors, solution providers, and authorized third parties are responsible for correctly implementing these design and communication standards.

### 2. Brand Identity Components
The identity must be consistently implemented using:
*   One official logo.
*   One color palette and typography.
*   One set of positioning statements.
*   One company website: **www.sambeconsulting.co.za**.
*   One set of official collateral (brochures, datasheets, white papers, etc.).
*   One official PowerPoint template (supplemented by event-specific templates).
*   One stationery design (business cards, letterheads, envelopes, etc.).

### 4. Typography Guidelines
*   **Official Font:** The official typeface for all documents and emails created by employees is **Verdana**.
*   **Uniformity:** Use a consistent type weight and point size suited for normal reading distances.
*   **Layout:**
    *   Maintain uniform column widths within a single item.
    *   Avoid mixing justified and unjustified columns or varying the number of columns per page.
    *   Standard typesetting is **justified on the left** and **unjustified on the right**.
    *   New paragraphs must be indicated uniformly by either a line space or an indentation.
*   **Emphasis:** Use a fine line to **underline** or use **bold** text. Do not underline headings or bullet points. Avoid overusing emphasis to prevent a cluttered look.
*   **Backgrounds:** Do not use tinted graphics, symbols, or text effects behind text as they are distracting.
*   **Tone:** The writing style should be clear, confident, and simple—no gimmicks, fuss, or clutter.

### 5. Logo Usage Guidelines
#### Correct Usage
*   **Form:** Always use the current logo in its complete form.
*   **Size:** Minimum width of **4cm** for print or **108 pixels** for web.
*   **Proportion:** The logo should not exceed **15%** of a page or area, except for office signage or tradeshow booths.
*   **Clearance (Exclusion Zone):** Maintain an equal exclusion zone on all four sides where no text or graphics can encroach. The minimum space (**X**) is equal to **1/2 of the logo height**.
*   **Production:** The logo may be blind embossed or foil stamped. Special materials (like plastic) require marketing approval for specific tooling versions.

#### Incorrect Usage
*   **Distortion:** Do not stretch or condense the logo in only one direction. Do not tilt or position it at an angle.
*   **Crowding:** Do not force the logo into a space that is too small or crowded.
*   **Containment:** Do not place the logo inside a box or circle to make it look like part of the logo.
*   **Text Integration:** Do not use the logo as part of a sentence. Instead, type "Sambe Consulting" in plain text.
*   **New Logos:** Do not associate the logo with special fonts to create "new" logos for specific programs.
*   **Reproduction:** Only use official logo files. Do not scan the logo from printed materials.
*   **Misrepresentation:** Do not use the logo to imply ownership of a third-party company or its products.
